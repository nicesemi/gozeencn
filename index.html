    I have Ehlers-Danlos and neurogenic POTS (due to small-fiber neuropathy), and have issues with standing for too long in one place. I am able to walk without much of a problem, I just cannot stop for more than a few moments without my blood falling down.

    Walking my dog was proving to be problematic during POTS cycles as he likes to stop and sniff around a lot, requiring me to swing my ByAcre walker behind me to sit, and then flick it forward again as we moved on, over and over and over again during our walks. Inefficient and tiring.

    When I stumbled upon the Zeen I was intrigued as it seemed as though it might be a great solution to my particular issue.

    From the moment it arrived- in the coolest shipping box I've ever seen- it has been in near constant use.

    It it obviously thoughtfully designed, and I appreciate all the little touches that make life just a tiny bit easier: the magnetized seatbelt straps that keep them close at hand, the reachable locking casters, a swivel seat that moves with a walker's natural hip roll, the fact that I am able to adjust everything on my own without any special tools.

    The Zeen itself feels well built and extremely sturdy. I can lean forward and pick up after my dog, clip on his lead, or even tie my shoes without feeling as though I am going to tip over. The seat and back are comfortable enough to sit for a long time. It also fits through all of my doorways without issue.

    Also, "Zeening" (cruising) is the most fun I've had in a while! I am able to move at a good clip... certainly faster than I normally move, haha.

    I also love that their company has been so responsive to my questions. They reached out to me first about any questions or problems I might have from the moment the Zeen was delivered.

    When I had a small issue with one of the straps, they sent me a whole new seat back immediately. They also walked me through changing the piston level through Zoom, and even watched my gate to ensure I had everything adjusted correctly.

    I am really hoping now for a phone holder that attaches to the handle, and some shopping cart/stroller clips. I am excited to see what they do next!

    (This is my honest review. I am not affiliated with the company in any way. I do not receive anything for my reviews, and I do not have or use any referral codes. The Zeen was purchased for its full price, in its entirety by me.)
